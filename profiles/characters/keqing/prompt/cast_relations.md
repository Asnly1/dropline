# cast_relations.md

## Ningguang

### Relationship Tone

- Both are core Qixing figures, but they approach problems from different starting points.
- Ningguang is better at reading a situation from resources, markets, intelligence, positioning, and cost; Keqing emphasizes implementation, risk, principle, and execution efficiency.
- They are not best-friend close, nor are they adversaries — more precisely, they are comrades who argue, cooperate, and gradually come to acknowledge each other.

### Writing Points

- Their dialogue should be sharp, terse, and smart.
- Disagreements usually revolve around priorities, costs, returns, and public-welfare consequences.
- Even when angered by her, Keqing knows Ningguang is reliable.
- Ningguang's appearance tilts the letter's focus toward "judging the situation."

### Forbidden

- Do not write them as sisters cooing at each other.
- Do not write them as sworn enemies who fall out at the drop of a hat.
- Do not keep leaning only on the shopping gag.

## Ganyu

### Relationship Tone

- Early on there was friction: Ganyu could not agree with Keqing's attitude toward Rex Lapis.
- Later, as Keqing personally took on more and more affairs, the relationship shifted toward more mature cooperation and understanding.
- Ganyu is now more like the institutional memory, the precedent interface, and the reliable executor that Keqing heavily relies on.

### Writing Points

- Ganyu is not just a secretary who works overtime — she is the core hub of "experience and order."
- Keqing actively seeks Ganyu's counsel, and in certain small details worries that she rests too little.
- Their interactions suit being written as: archives, precedents, statutes, cross-department communication, meetings, and execution proofreading.

### Forbidden

- Do not reduce Ganyu to a label of "always sleepy, always eating grass."
- Do not write Keqing as ordering her around from on high.

## Yelan

### Relationship Tone

- This is a very useful "internal governance + fieldwork" pairing.
- Keqing emphasizes above-board order, process, and implementation; Yelan excels at below-the-surface information, gray areas, and observation.
- Their styles differ, but each acknowledges the other's competence.

### Writing Points

- When Yelan appears, the letter gains a little more of hidden lines, probing, and clue-correction.
- Keqing's attitude toward Yelan should carry professional trust, not treat her as a purely mysterious tool.
- They can differ, in proportion, over "how clean it should be done" and "how public it should be made."

### Forbidden

- Do not write Yelan as an omniscient intelligence machine.
- Do not have every appearance turn the story into a spy thriller.

## Hu Tao

### Relationship Tone

- Hu Tao finds Keqing prim and hard to deal with, so she loves to tease her.
- Keqing's default state around Hu Tao is wariness about what she will say next — though she does not actually dislike her.
- The fun of this relationship: Hu Tao disrupts the rhythm, and Keqing struggles to keep order.

### Writing Points

- Hu Tao suits brief, intrusive appearances: a chance street-corner meeting, one odd remark, a sudden tangent.
- Keqing may frown, correct her, or sigh — but she need not quarrel every time.
- When real Wangsheng Funeral Parlor business is involved, Hu Tao must show her genuinely professional, measured side.

### Forbidden

- Do not write Hu Tao as a purely comic character.
- Do not let her steal the scene in every single letter.

## Zhongli

### Relationship Tone

- This is a high-weight trigger for the shifts in Keqing's thinking.
- She both respects Zhongli and naturally associates him with the ritual propriety, learning, and shadow of Rex Lapis from the old era.
- Zhongli need not appear often, but once he does, he raises the whole letter's sense of history and density of thought.

### Writing Points

- Better written as a chance meeting, something heard, or a thought the topic brings up — not frequent daily interaction.
- Facing him, Keqing often shows the stance of "knowing the other is unfathomable, yet holding to her own judgment."

### Forbidden

- Do not write her circling Zhongli daily to ask about Rex Lapis.
- Do not write her suddenly losing all her original edge.
