# cast_pool.md

## Usage Principles

- It is not "the more characters the better," but "the more functional the appearance the better."
- A letter defaults to letting only 0–2 supporting characters appear explicitly.
- Supporting characters exist to add affairs, atmosphere, and world-feel — not to steal the lead.

## Tier 1: High-Frequency Relationship Axis

These people most easily affect Keqing's judgment and mood directly.

- Ningguang: budget, priorities, resource allocation, policy judgment
- Ganyu: precedent, archives, meetings, execution proofreading
- Yelan: intelligence, fieldwork, gray-area clues
- Hu Tao: disrupting the rhythm, sudden interruptions, slipping from a joke into real business
- Zhongli: a sense of history, ritual propriety, echoes of the old order

## Tier 2: Task Support Circle

These people suit advancing concrete events.

- Yanfei: legal interpretation, compliance, statute revision, consultation
- Beidou: harbor, trade routes, shipping, the edges of the rules
- Xingqiu: the Feiyun Commerce Guild, scion of a prominent family, an investigation line that combines bookishness with drive
- Chongyun: light mystery, exorcism mix-ups, occasional special incidents
- Baizhu: the body, prescriptions, treatment, fatigue and recovery
- Lan Yan: local crafts, Chenyu Vale, human warmth, folk handiwork

## Tier 3: Daily Life Circle

These people are not the main axis, but they are very important, because they keep Liyue "alive."

- Xiangling: food, liveliness, the warmth of daily life, banquet preparation
- Yun Jin: opera, festivals, public occasions, social face
- Gaming: escort and freight, the streets, lion dance, festive-season liveliness, the aftermath of freight safety
- Xinyan: a non-traditional but energetic side of Liyue

## Tier 4: Low-Frequency Heavyweight Circle

Low frequency but weighty; once they appear, the mood grows heavier.

- Xiao: old duty, guardianship, the boundary between humans and adepti
- Xianyun: machinery, an adeptus's perspective, a new life among mortals
- Shenhe: cold, otherworldly, appears only in very small doses
- Yaoyao: caretaking, gentle transitions, the everyday side
- Qiqi: the herb hut, memory, an odd stillness
- Cizhu: the aftermath of a newer-era adeptus/legend, the wilds, edification, an old will like the ley lines

## Tier 5: Background Flavor Circle

These characters need not be permanent, but can be touched on occasionally to make the world more complete.

- people under Ningguang, commerce-guild members, Ministry of Civil Affairs staff, the Millelith, local figures of Chenyu Vale, and so on
- this tier mostly does not need named appearances — a passing mention is enough

## Appearance Frequency Rules

- Tier 1: high frequency, but at most 1 person appears explicitly per letter
- Tier 2: medium frequency, suited to being tied to affairs
- Tier 3: medium-low frequency, mainly for life-feel
- Tier 4: low frequency; once they appear, make the mood weightier
- Tier 5: use as needed, never stealing the scene
